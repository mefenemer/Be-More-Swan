How one assistant works

Brief it once, then let it get on with it. You only step in to say yes, no, or “not quite”.

1

Brief it like a new starter

Tell it about your business, what you sell, who your customers are and how you like to sound — the conversation you’d have with anyone joining your team on their first day. It takes about ten to fifteen minutes.

Then decide the rhythm: three posts a week, an article a fortnight, a newsletter every month.

2

It does the work. You say yes.

Your assistant drafts on its own, in the background, whether you’re logged in or not. Every draft then waits for you, shown exactly as your customers will see it.

Approve it, change a word, or send it back with a note. Nothing goes out until you’re happy.

3

It learns how you like things

Every time you turn a draft down and say why, your assistant turns that into a rule it follows next time. The first week, you’ll make a few changes. A month in, you’ll mostly be pressing Approve.

When you trust it, you can let some work go out on its own — and switch that off again whenever you like.

Better together

One assistant saves you hours. A team changes how your business runs. Your assistants share what they know — your products, your brand, and what the rest of the team has done this week — so they build on each other’s work instead of repeating it.

One goal, the whole team

Give it a goal, not a to-do list

Tell your Marketing Campaign Orchestrator what you want — “win 25 new wholesale accounts before Christmas” — and it plans the campaign and briefs the rest of the team: leads to find, posts to write, emails to send.

You decide how much of your monthly allowance it can use, and it asks before going further.

Spotted by one, fixed by another

Assistants hand work to each other

Your Mystery Shopper reads your website the way a stranger would and tells you exactly what to change. When the fix is a post, an article or an email, it suggests the job for the assistant who does that — with the brief already written for you to send.

And when a lead your Lead Generation Assistant found becomes a customer, they can join your mailing list, so your Email Marketing Assistant keeps them coming back.
