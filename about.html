The Founder's Story

From the Corporate Grind to the Aegean Sea

Chapter One · The Grind

25 Years Inside the Machine

Our founder spent 25 years in high-stakes project and portfolio management — steering massive enterprise budgets, leading sprawling cross-functional teams, and delivering programmes where failure wasn't an option.

From the inside, a pattern became impossible to ignore. The traditional operational model was broken. Brilliant people spent their days feeding status reports, chasing updates, reconciling spreadsheets, and servicing the machinery of work instead of doing the work itself.

The result was universal: burnout. Not because people weren't capable, but because the model demanded that humans behave like software — repetitive, always-on, endlessly available. Something had to change.

Chapter Two · The Escape

Building Be More Swan from Syros

The realisation behind Be More Swan was simple: automation shouldn't be a privilege reserved for massive corporations with in-house engineering armies. Solo founders and small teams deserve the same invisible workforce — without writing a line of code.

So the corporate ladder got traded for a laptop on the Greek island of Syros. Today, Be More Swan is a global tech platform run from the Aegean — living proof that with the right digital workforce, true location and time freedom aren't a fantasy. They're an operating model.

The swan is our promise. Serene on the surface, powerful underneath. Your business should glide — and your digital team should do the furious paddling below the waterline.
