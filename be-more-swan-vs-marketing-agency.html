When an agency or freelancer is the better choice

We’d rather you pick the right option than the wrong one with us.

Hire people if:

  • You need a full rebrand, a big campaign idea or a video shoot. Creative strategy and production are what good agencies do best.
  • You’d rather not look at anything. Our assistants draft and you approve. If you want someone else to make every decision, an agency suits you better.
  • You’re spending heavily on paid ads. Managing large ad budgets day to day is specialist work.

You can also use both: an agency for the big creative moments, and Be More Swan for the everyday posts, articles, emails and leads that keep your business visible in between.

Common questions

Is Be More Swan an agency?

No. Be More Swan is a web app that gives you a team of AI assistants. They do the day-to-day marketing work, and you approve everything before it goes out.

Will it sound like me, or like a robot?

Each assistant learns your business from your setup answers, your website and any brand documents you add, and writes in your voice. When you turn a draft down and say why, it turns that into a rule it follows next time.

Does anything get published without me seeing it?

Not by default. Every post, article and email waits for your approval. Once you trust an assistant, you can choose to let some work go out on its own, and switch that off again whenever you like.

Which platforms does it post to?

Our Connections page lists which platforms are live today and which are coming soon.

What if I want to stop?

Cancel any time from your billing page. There is no contract or notice period.

Can I use Be More Swan and an agency together?

Yes. Some businesses use an agency for big creative work such as a rebrand or a campaign shoot, and Be More Swan for the everyday posts, articles, emails and leads in between.
