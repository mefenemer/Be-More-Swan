When Wix or Squarespace is the better choice

We’d rather you pick the right tool than the wrong one with us.

Choose a traditional website builder if:

  • You want full control over the design. If you love placing every element yourself, a drag-and-drop editor gives you more freedom than we do.
  • You need a large site. Our plans cover up to 5 or 15 pages. A big catalogue or a site with dozens of pages needs a different tool.
  • You rely on a particular app or plugin. Their marketplaces have many more add-ons than we do.

If what you want is a clear, professional website without writing it yourself, plus help growing the business behind it, that’s what we’re built for.

Common questions

Do I need to know how to build a website?

No. There is no code and no drag-and-drop. You describe what you want, or edit the words directly, and the layout is handled for you.

Can I move my site over from Wix or Squarespace?

Yes, if it is your own. Give the Website Assistant the address of your current site, whether it is on Wix, Squarespace, WordPress or built for you, and it reads your home page and the pages in its menu into a draft. Your own words are kept as they are. You see the imported draft beside your current one and choose whether to use it.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. When you publish, your site goes live on its own free address, yourname.bemoreswan.site, or on a domain you own.

How many pages can I have?

Up to 5 pages on The Workflow Saver plan and 15 on The Busywork Buster.

Will it make up reviews or prices?

No. It only states what is in your business details and products. Anything else, such as a customer quote or a number, is left as a [placeholder] for you to fill in.

What does the Business Mentor do?

Your Business Mentor is a guide and coach included on every plan. It looks across your workspace, tells you what it would sort out first, helps you plan and checks your week with you. It never changes anything without your click.

Is the Business Mentor included on the cheaper plan?

Yes. Every plan includes a Business Mentor, and it doesn’t take up your plan’s assistant place. On The Workflow Saver you choose one assistant; choose the Website Assistant and you get your website and your Mentor.

Does it use tasks?

Drafting your whole site, importing your current site, or having it place your own words on a page each uses 1 task from your monthly allowance. Editing pages yourself, and approving changes it suggests in chat, costs nothing.
