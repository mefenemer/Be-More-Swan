Reviews

Real reviews from your customers — from Google, Facebook, an email or a card. Copy them word for word and say where they came from; your website quotes them from here. Never add a review you made up or paid for: it's against UK law.

Add a review

Hidden reviews aren't quoted on your website but still count in your average, so it stays honest. Tip: you can also paste several reviews into your Website Assistant's chat and it will set them out for you to check.

Ask customers for a review

One friendly email, sent once, after someone's visit or order through your website. They write the review on your own site; you approve it before it appears. Nobody is asked twice in 30 days, and anyone can say "don't ask me again".

If you add it, every customer is also offered it — whatever they thought. (Google doesn't allow sending only happy customers.) Find it in your Google Business Profile under "Ask for reviews".
