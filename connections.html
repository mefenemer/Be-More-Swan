Campaign Assistant

Needs no connections of its own. It briefs your other assistants on one shared goal, and they use their connections to do the work.

Connected, but always in your control

Each assistant sees only what it needs

A connection is only ever used for its assistant's job. Your Social Media Assistant can't reach your CRM, and your Minute Taker can't post to your social accounts.

Nothing goes out without your OK

Posts, articles and emails wait for your approval by default. You decide if anything should go out on its own.

Disconnect any time

Remove a connection from the Connections tab whenever you like, and the assistant stops using it straight away. Your plan sets how many apps you can connect.
