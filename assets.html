Your calendar

Works with Google, Outlook and Apple calendars.

1. See your bookings in your calendar

Add this private link to your calendar app as a subscribed calendar. Bookings, class dates and who's coming appear there. Google updates it every few hours (sometimes up to a day); Apple and Outlook more often — your booking emails stay the instant notice.

Open in Apple / Outlook
How to add it
  • Google Calendar (on a computer): Other calendars ▸ + ▸ From URL ▸ paste the link ▸ Add calendar.
  • Outlook: Add calendar ▸ Subscribe from web ▸ paste the link.
  • Apple Calendar: click "Open in Apple / Outlook", or File ▸ New Calendar Subscription.

Keep it private — anyone with the link can see your bookings. (the old one stops working).

2. Don't take bookings when you're busy

Paste your calendar's private address in iCal format. Times you're busy there can't be booked; we check every 15 minutes. Only the start and end of each event are read — never titles or who's invited. Events marked "free" are ignored.

Where to find it
  • Google Calendar: Settings ▸ your calendar ▸ Integrate calendar ▸ Secret address in iCal format.
  • Outlook: Settings ▸ Calendar ▸ Shared calendars ▸ Publish a calendar ▸ ICS link.
  • Apple / iCloud: the calendar's ⓘ ▸ Public Calendar ▸ copy the link.

Each person in "Who or what can be booked" can have their own calendar too — it then blocks only their bookings.
