2. Becoming a partner

2.1 You apply on our partner page. We review every application and may approve or decline it at our discretion. You must be at least 18 and able to enter a contract.

2.2 Once approved you get a personal invite link, and we may also give you a memorable partner code. Keep your account details secure; you are responsible for activity on your partner account.

3. How you earn commission

3.1 — Who counts. You earn commission when someone signs up through your invite link or partner code, or enters your code when choosing a plan, and then becomes a paying customer. They must be new to Be More Swan: you do not earn on existing or past paying customers, on yourself, on accounts you or your business control, or on someone already introduced by another person. The first introduction we record is the one that counts.

3.2 — How much. Commission is a percentage of what the customer actually pays us, excluding VAT, for the commission period. Your Partner page shows the rate and period that apply to new customers.

3.3 — Rates are fixed per customer. A customer earns you the rate and period in force on the day they first pay. We may change the rate for future customers by giving notice; a change never reduces the rate on customers who have already paid.

3.4 — Free periods earn nothing. Customers you introduce get a free trial and their first paid month free. Commission starts from the first payment that takes money.

4. When and how you are paid

4.1 — Holding period. Each commission waits for a holding period after the customer's payment (currently 30 days) so that refunds can settle. It then becomes ready to pay.

4.2 — Monthly payouts. We pay ready-to-pay commission in a monthly payout run, once you have at least the minimum (currently £50) and have confirmed your payout details. Smaller balances roll over. We pay in pounds sterling by PayPal, Wise or transfer to a UK bank account.

4.3 — Refunds and disputes. If a customer's payment is refunded or disputed, the commission on it is reduced or cancelled. If we have already paid it, the amount is deducted from your future payouts.

4.4 — Your payout details. You set them on your Partner page. For your security, a change only takes effect when you confirm it from the email we send you. We are not responsible for a payment made to details you gave us.

4.5 — Tax. You are responsible for declaring and paying any tax on your commission. Commission is stated inclusive of any VAT you may owe on it. If you are VAT-registered, tell us your VAT number before your first payout and we will agree how VAT is invoiced.
