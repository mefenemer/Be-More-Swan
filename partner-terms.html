3. How you earn commission

3.1 — Who counts. You earn commission when someone signs up through your invite link or partner code, or enters your code when choosing a plan, and then becomes a paying customer. They must be new to Be More Swan: you do not earn on existing or past paying customers, on yourself, on accounts you or your business control, or on someone already introduced by another person. The first introduction we record is the one that counts, and our records decide who introduced a customer. You must raise any question about an introduction or a commission within 60 days of the payment it concerns.

3.2 — How much. Commission is worked out after costs. From what the customer actually pays us, excluding VAT, we first take a fixed cost deduction towards the cost of providing their service. Your commission rate then applies to what is left, for the commission period. The cost deduction is currently 20%. For example, if a customer pays £79 excluding VAT, £15.80 is deducted for costs, leaving £63.20; at a 25% rate you earn £15.80. Your Partner page shows the rate, cost deduction and period that apply to new customers, including any different rate we agree with you in writing.

3.3 — Rates are fixed per customer. A customer earns you the rate, cost deduction and period in force on the day they first pay. We may change these for future customers by giving you at least 30 days’ notice (see section 8); a change never reduces the rate, or increases the cost deduction, on customers who have already paid.

3.4 — Free periods earn nothing. Customers you introduce currently get a free trial and their first paid month free. Commission starts from the first payment that takes money.

4. When and how you are paid

4.1 — Holding period. Each commission waits for a holding period after the customer's payment (currently 30 days) so that refunds can settle. It then becomes ready to pay.

4.2 — Monthly payouts. We aim to pay ready-to-pay commission once a month, by the end of the following month, once you have at least the minimum (currently £50), have accepted the current version of these terms and have confirmed your payout details. Smaller balances roll over. We pay in pounds sterling by PayPal, Wise or transfer to a UK bank account. Any currency conversion, and any fees charged by your bank, PayPal or Wise, are yours. If a customer pays in another currency, commission is worked out on the sterling amount we actually receive.

4.3 — Refunds, disputes and money owed back. If a customer’s payment is refunded, charged back or disputed, or we reasonably find that a commission was earned in breach of these terms, the commission on it is reduced or cancelled. If we have already paid it, we may deduct the amount from any sum we owe you; any amount not recovered within 60 days is a debt you must repay to us on demand. A commission cancelled because of a dispute is reinstated if we win the dispute.

4.4 — Your payout details. You set them on your Partner page. For your security, a change only takes effect when you confirm it from the email we send you. We are not responsible for a payment made to details you gave us.

4.5 — Tax. You are solely responsible for all taxes, social security contributions and registrations arising on your commission, wherever you are.
