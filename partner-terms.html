3. How you earn commission

3.1 — Who counts. You earn commission when someone signs up through your invite link or partner code, or enters your code when choosing a plan, and then becomes a paying customer. They must be new to Be More Swan: you do not earn on existing or past paying customers, on yourself, on accounts you or your business control, or on someone already introduced by another person. The first introduction we record is the one that counts, and our records decide who introduced a customer. You must raise any question about an introduction or a commission within 60 days of the payment it concerns.

3.2 — How much. Commission is earned only on Subscription Payments. A “Subscription Payment” is what a customer actually pays us, excluding VAT, for their recurring Be More Swan plan (monthly or annual), including a payment for upgrading that plan. It does not include payments for add-ons; task, credit or booster packs; top-ups; usage or overage charges; one-off purchases; set-up or service fees; third-party services; money we collect for or on behalf of the customer’s own customers (for example shop, booking, deposit or holiday-let payments); or any part of a payment covered by credit, vouchers or refer-a-friend rewards. Your commission is a percentage of each Subscription Payment, for the commission period. The standard commission is currently 20% of each Subscription Payment for the customer’s first 12 months of payments. For example, if a customer pays £69 excluding VAT, you earn £13.80. Your Partner page shows the commission and period that apply to new customers, including any different commission we agree with you in writing. The percentage is a fixed figure that we set; it is not worked out from, and is not adjusted for, our costs for any customer.

3.3 — Commission is fixed per customer. A customer earns you the commission percentage and period in force on the day they first pay. We may change these for future customers by giving you at least 30 days’ notice, or at least 60 days’ notice for a change that reduces your commission (see section 8); a change never reduces the commission on customers who have already paid. This protects the commission on a customer while your partnership continues. It does not stop commission ending under section 7.4.

3.4 — Free periods earn nothing. Customers you introduce currently get a free trial and their first paid month free. Commission starts from the first payment that takes money.

3.5 — Longest commission period and special commission. Unless a written commission agreement signed by a director of Be More Swan Ltd expressly refers to and overrides this section, no commission period is longer than 24 months from the customer’s first payment that takes money, and no commission is higher than 32% of a Subscription Payment, whatever your Partner page shows. Either of us may end a special commission on 3 months’ notice. Customers who first pay after the notice ends then earn the standard commission, and customers who first paid before keep the commission they locked in, subject to this section and section 7.4.

4. When and how you are paid

4.1 — Holding period. Each commission waits for a holding period after the customer's payment (currently 30 days) so that refunds can settle. It then becomes ready to pay.

4.2 — Monthly payouts. We aim to pay ready-to-pay commission once a month, by the end of the following month, once you have at least the minimum (currently £50), have accepted the current version of these terms and have confirmed your payout details. Smaller balances roll over, but if you have had ready-to-pay commission below the minimum for 6 months in a row, we pay it at the next payout run, provided it is at least £10 and you have confirmed your payout details. We pay in pounds sterling by PayPal, Wise or transfer to a UK bank account. Any currency conversion, and any fees charged by your bank, PayPal or Wise, are yours. Commission is earned only on payments a customer makes to us in pounds sterling.

4.3 — Refunds, disputes and money owed back. If a customer’s payment is refunded, charged back or disputed, or we reasonably find that a commission was earned in breach of these terms, the commission on it is reduced or cancelled. If we have already paid it, we may deduct the amount from any sum we owe you; any amount not recovered within 60 days is a debt you must repay to us on demand. A commission cancelled because of a dispute is reinstated if we win the dispute.

4.4 — Your payout details. You set them on your Partner page. For your security, a change only takes effect when you confirm it from the email we send you. We are not responsible for a payment made to details you gave us.

4.5 — Tax. You are solely responsible for all taxes, social security contributions and registrations arising on your commission, wherever you are. Commission is stated inclusive of any VAT or similar tax you may owe on it. If you are registered for VAT, tell us your VAT number before your first payout, and give us any tax details or forms we reasonably ask for. We may deduct any tax we are required by law to withhold. We may refuse or delay a payment to anyone subject to sanctions, or where making it would breach anti-money-laundering or sanctions law.
