Where Be More Swan wins

The words are done for you

The hard part of a website is knowing what to say. Your Website Assistant drafts every page from what it already knows about your business: headings, wording, the questions buyers ask and a clear next step, in plain British English.

It never makes things up

It only states what’s in your business details and products. Where a page needs a fact only you know, like a customer quote or how long you’ve been trading, it leaves a clear [placeholder], and the editor lists every one left before you publish.

Changes don’t cost you credits

Edit the words yourself, or ask in chat and see exactly what will change before anything is saved. Editing and approving changes is free. A whole-site draft or an import uses 1 task from your monthly allowance.

When another builder is the better choice

We’d rather you pick the right tool than the wrong one with us.

  • Squarespace, if design is everything. Its templates are polished, and you get fine control over how every page looks.
  • Wix, if you want to place every element yourself. Its drag-and-drop editor gives you more layout freedom than we do, and its app market has many more add-ons.
  • Webador, if the lowest price matters most. Its paid plans start at a few pounds a month, and it’s simple to use when you’re happy to write your own words.
  • Lovable, if you’re building an app, not a business website. Logins, databases and custom features are what it’s for, and you can take the code away with you on a paid plan.

If what you want is a clear, professional website without writing it yourself, plus help growing the business behind it, that’s what we’re built for.

Common questions

Is the Website Assistant a website builder?

Yes, but it works the other way round. Instead of handing you a template to fill, it writes every page from your business details, products and brand kit, shows you a live preview on a desktop or a phone, and publishes the site when you’re happy.

Can I move my site over from Squarespace, Wix or another builder?

Yes, if it is your own. Give the Website Assistant the address of your current site, whether it is on Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, GoDaddy or another builder, made with an AI tool like Lovable, or built for you. It brings your home page and the pages in its menu into a draft: your words exactly as they are, your pictures, your menu, and your colours and fonts. If you move your domain over, your old page addresses lead to the new pages. You choose whether to use the imported draft. Pages behind a login, a working cart and add-on widgets such as chat boxes can't come across; your shop is rebuilt with checkout on your own Stripe account.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. When you publish, your site goes live on its own free address, yourname.bemoreswan.site, or on a domain you own.

How many pages can I have?

As many as your site needs. No plan limits how many pages your website has.

Can I sell online and take bookings?

Yes. Your Website Assistant can add a shop, with checkout on your own Stripe account and no cut taken by us (Stripe’s card fees apply), and bookings for appointments, classes, events and holiday-let stays, with deposits, reminders and sync with your Google or Outlook calendar. Appointments can be in person, by phone or on a video call with your own Zoom, Google Meet or Teams link, and you get a booking link of your own to share. Prices and services come from your Business Information, so they are never made up.

How do the Mystery Shopper and Website Assistant work together?

Every week the Mystery Shopper scores your site against up to three competitors and suggests changes, with the wording written. Choose to hand a change to your Website Assistant and it shows you exactly what it will change; nothing is saved until you approve it. After you publish, the Mystery Shopper checks the change is live. This works for sites hosted with Be More Swan, on a plan with both assistants.

Which plan do I need?

The Workflow Saver includes one assistant plus your Business Mentor. Choose the Website Assistant and you get your website, shop, bookings, forms and reviews. For the Mystery Shopper, your blog at /blog and the rest of the team working together, choose The Busywork Buster.

How is this different from Lovable?

Lovable is an AI tool for building web apps from prompts, and each change you ask for uses credits. The Website Assistant is made for small business websites: it already knows your business, never makes up facts, and editing your pages yourself or approving changes it suggests costs nothing. If you have already built your business site with Lovable, the Website Assistant can import it.

Does it use tasks?

Drafting your whole site, importing your current site, or having it place your own words on a page each uses 1 task from your monthly allowance. Editing pages yourself, and approving changes it suggests in chat, costs nothing. Starter pictures made by AI use tasks too; stock photos don’t.
