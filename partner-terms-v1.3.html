3. How you earn commission

3.1 — Who counts. You earn commission when someone signs up through your invite link or partner code, or enters your code when choosing a plan, and then becomes a paying customer. They must be new to Be More Swan: you do not earn on existing or past paying customers, on yourself, on accounts you or your business control, or on someone already introduced by another person. You must raise any question about an introduction or a commission within 60 days of the date it first appears, or should have appeared, on your Partner page or a payout statement, unless you could not reasonably have noticed the problem in that time.

3.2 — How much. Commission is earned only on Subscription Payments. A “Subscription Payment” is what a customer actually pays us, excluding VAT, sales tax, use tax and any similar tax, for their recurring Be More Swan plan (monthly or annual), including a payment for upgrading that plan. It does not include payments for add-ons; task, credit or booster packs; top-ups; usage or overage charges; one-off purchases; set-up or service fees; third-party services; money we collect for or on behalf of the customer’s own customers (for example shop, booking, deposit or holiday-let payments); or any part of a payment covered by credit, vouchers or refer-a-friend rewards. If a customer pays in a currency other than pounds sterling, the Subscription Payment is the amount in pounds sterling that our payment provider converts that payment into for us, at its rate on the day of the payment. Your commission is a percentage of each Subscription Payment made during the commission period. The standard commission is currently 20% of each Subscription Payment made in the 12 months starting on the date of the customer’s first payment that takes money. For example, if a customer pays £69 excluding VAT, you earn £13.80. Where a Subscription Payment covers a period that runs beyond the customer’s commission period (for example an annual plan bought late in it), commission is earned only on the part of that payment for the time inside the commission period, worked out pro rata. Your Partner page shows the commission and period that apply to new customers, including any different commission we agree with you in writing. The percentage is a fixed figure that we set; it is not worked out from, and is not adjusted for, our costs for any customer.

3.3 — Commission is fixed per customer. A customer earns you the commission percentage and period in force on the day they first pay. We may change these for future customers by giving you at least 30 days’ notice, or at least 60 days’ notice for a change that reduces your commission (see section 8); a change never reduces the commission on customers who have already paid. This protects the commission on a customer while your partnership continues. It does not stop commission ending under section 7.4.

3.4 — Free periods earn nothing. Customers you introduce currently get a free trial and their first paid month free. Commission starts from the first payment that takes money.

3.5 — Longest commission period and special commission. Customers who first pay after the notice ends then earn the standard commission, and customers who first paid before keep the commission they locked in, subject to this section and section 7.4.

Smaller balances roll over, but if you have had ready-to-pay commission below the minimum for 6 months in a row, we pay it at the next payout run, provided it is at least £10 and you have confirmed your payout details. Commission is worked out and paid in pounds sterling, by PayPal, Wise, transfer to a UK bank account, or transfer to a US bank account through Wise. For a US bank account, Wise converts the sterling amount to US dollars at its exchange rate on the day of the payout, so the dollar amount you receive can vary from one payout to the next. We pay the fee our payment provider charges us to send a payout. Any fee your bank, PayPal or Wise charges you to receive it, and any conversion of the amount we send into another currency, is yours. With each payout we give you a statement of the commission it pays.
