# Recall.ai support reply, 26 November 2026

Reply from Recall support (Ben, via recall@service.usepylon.com) to our email of 25 November 2026
(questions in `docs/note-taker-launch-notices-draft.md`). Summary of what Recall confirmed:

1. **Model training.** Recall and its transcription sub-processors do not use our customers'
   meeting audio, recordings or transcripts to train or fine-tune AI models. This applies to all
   our workspaces (EU and US). The standard DPA (recall.ai/data-processing-agreement) applies
   automatically to our pay-as-you-go account.
2. **Breach notice.** DPA §8.6: Recall notifies us of a covered personal data breach "without
   undue delay". There is no fixed 48-hour deadline; a fixed commitment would need a talk with
   Recall's account executive. Our own DPA's 48 hours run from when we become aware, so the
   standard terms are enough.
3. **Speaker labels.** In our `recallai_streaming` setup, speaker labels come from the meeting
   platform's participant names (docs.recall.ai/docs/diarization#speaker-labels). Recall does not
   use voice-based identification.
