## Verdict

**GO for Phase 1, with three conditions.** Written 2026-11-15. Phase 0 cost $0.81 in total, all of it from
DataForSEO's free $1 trial.

**1. The data holds up for the terms customers would track.**
- **UK: 60/60 usable. US: 57/60. Scotland: 51/60.**
- The earlier 5-term test gave Wales 3/5 and Texas 4/5. Replies are in `.tmp-trends-phase0/`; Wales and Texas are
  not in this table.
- Use the country by default. Offer a nation or state per term only when that term passes there.
- Terms that are thin in Scotland are trades with few searches: kitchen fitter, bathroom renovation, event catering,
  cat sitter, music lessons, emergency plumber. These need the plain "not enough searches to chart" message from the plan.

**2. Most seasons are right, but the busiest *week* is noisy. Report the busiest *months*.**
- Examples that are clearly right: tax return in January, florist in March (Mother's Day), loft insulation in
  November, boiler service and roofer in October, glamping and holiday cottage in July, takeaway in December.
- Some single-week peaks are chance: storage unit in December, printing in February.
- Phase 1 should average the weeks into months. It should call a season only when the busiest months stand clearly
  above the yearly average, and say nothing otherwise.

**3. Rising queries need a relevance filter before any customer sees one.**
- **2,506 rising queries.** 88% share a word with their term, but that still lets through junk:
  - brands: "oak furnitureland"
  - news: "ford electrician fired"
  - trivia: "which greek philosopher served as the tutor of alexander the great?"
  - other towns: "emergency plumber milton keynes"
- **Only 5% are phrased as questions,** e.g. "how much does a boiler service cost" or "how many driving lessons do you
  need uk". Those are the best "Write about this" ideas.
- Phase 1 needs a cheap Haiku pass per term each week. It should keep queries a business in that trade could write
  about, and turn place-specific ones into the customer's own area or drop them.

**Cost check.** The Standard price is confirmed at $0.0027 a task on a real call. Answers took 1–4 minutes, not
45. The §6 estimate (≈ $27 a month at 300 workspaces) stands.
