**Subject:** Jobseeker API: is this use allowed, or do we need a commercial agreement?

Hello,

I run Be More Swan, a UK software company. We make AI assistants for small businesses, including a Lead
Generator that helps a business find other businesses it could sell to. We'd like to use the Reed Jobseeker API
for that, and want to check with you first that it is allowed.

**What we would do**
- Search for jobs by keyword and location: for example, "bookkeeper" near Leeds, or "chef" in Manchester.
- From each result, read only the employer's name, the job title, the location, the date posted and the link to the
  job on reed.co.uk.
- Tell one of our customers that a local employer is hiring for a role that is relevant to them, with the link
  back to the job on Reed. For example, a payroll company is told that a local café is recruiting its first staff.
  An employer that is itself a recruitment agency would be left out.

**What we would not do**
- We would not republish job adverts, show the full description, or build a job board.
- We would not collect, store or use any information about job seekers.
- We would not contact anyone through Reed or apply to jobs.
- We would keep only what we need to show the link (employer, title, location, date), and delete it after 90 days.

**Volumes:** roughly a few hundred searches a day across all our customers at first, well within any rate limit you
set. We would keep within whatever limits and attribution you ask for, and credit Reed beside every result.

**Our questions**
1. Is this use permitted under the Jobseeker API terms with a free key?
2. If not, is there a commercial or partner arrangement that would cover it, and who should we speak to?
3. Is there anything you would want us to do differently: wording, attribution, limits?

We won't build any of this until we hear back from you, and we're happy to adjust it to suit Reed.
