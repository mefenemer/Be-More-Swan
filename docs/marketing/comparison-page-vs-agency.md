## Where an agency or freelancer is the better choice

We'd rather you pick the right option than the wrong one with us. Hire people if:

- **You need a full rebrand, a big campaign idea or a video shoot.** Creative strategy and
  production are what good agencies are best at.
- **You'd rather not look at anything.** Our assistants draft and you approve. If you want
  someone else to make every decision, an agency suits you better.
- **You're spending heavily on paid ads.** Managing large ad budgets day to day is specialist work.

You can also use both: an agency for the big creative moments, and Be More Swan for the
everyday posts, blogs, emails and leads that keep a business visible between them.

## Common questions

**Is Be More Swan an agency?**
No. It's a web app that gives you a team of AI assistants. They do the day-to-day marketing work,
and you approve everything before it goes out.

**Will it sound like me, or like a robot?**
Each assistant learns your business from your setup answers, your website and any brand documents
you add, and writes in your voice. You can edit anything before it goes out.

**Does anything get published without me seeing it?**
Not by default. Everything waits in your review queue. Once you trust an assistant, you can choose
to let it publish on its own.

**Which platforms does it post to?**
See our [Connections page](/connections) for what's live today and what's coming soon.

**What if I want to stop?**
Cancel any time from your billing page. There's no contract or notice period.

**How much does it cost?**
The Workflow Saver is £29 a month for one assistant. The Busywork Buster is £69 a month for every
assistant, working as one team. Both start with a 14-day free trial, no card needed.
