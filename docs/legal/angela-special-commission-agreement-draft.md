## 1. What this agreement is

1.1 You are a partner under the Be More Swan Partner Terms (version [1.3]), which you have accepted (**"the Partner
Terms"**). This agreement sets a special commission for you. Words with capital letters have the meaning they have in
the Partner Terms.

1.2 This agreement is a written commission agreement signed by a director of Be More Swan Ltd for the purposes of
**clause 3.5 of the Partner Terms**, and it **expressly refers to and overrides clause 3.5** to the extent set out in
clause 2. In everything else, the Partner Terms apply. If this agreement and the Partner Terms conflict, this agreement
applies.

## 2. Your special commission

2.1 **Who it applies to.** Customers you introduce who make their **first payment that takes money between
27 October 2026 and 26 October 2027** (inclusive, UK time) (**"Special Customers"**).

2.2 **How much.** For each Special Customer, you earn **50% of the Subscription Payment after costs**, where for each
month of service:

- **costs** are **the higher of** (a) **20%** of that month's Subscription Payment, or (b) the **Cost of Service** for
  that customer in that month, as defined in the Partner Terms (including AI model usage, image, video and voice
  generation, meeting note-taker minutes, email sending and card processing fees, as recorded by our systems); and
- if the Cost of Service in a month is more than that month's Subscription Payment, the difference is **carried
  forward** and taken off that customer's following months before your commission is worked out. Commission is never
  less than zero, and you never owe us anything because of costs. Any amount still carried forward when that
  customer's commission period ends falls away.

2.3 **For how long.** For each Special Customer, you earn this commission on their Subscription Payments for the
**first 12 months** from their first payment that takes money. Clause 3.5's 24-month limit is not exceeded.

2.4 **The cap.** Clause 3.5's limit that no commission is higher than 32% of a Subscription Payment **does not apply**
to your special commission. Your commission on a Subscription Payment will never be more than 40% of it (50% of the
80% left after the 20% minimum).

2.5 **Customers outside 2.1.** Customers who first pay before 27 October 2026 or after 26 October 2027 earn the
commission in the Partner Terms at the time they first pay, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

## 3. Prices and costs

3.1 We set and may change our prices, plans, features, usage limits and offers at any time. Your commission is always
a share of what a Special Customer actually pays us, worked out as in clause 2. If we raise or lower prices, your
commission follows what the customer actually pays.

3.2 The 20% minimum in clause 2.2(a) is fixed for Special Customers for the life of this agreement. The Cost of
Service is measured each month from our records. Your Partner page shows, for each payment, the payment, the costs
taken off, any amount carried forward, and your commission. You may query any figure under clause 3.1 of the Partner
Terms.

## 4. Ending

4.1 Either of us may end this special commission for **future** customers on 3 months' written notice (clause 3.5 of
the Partner Terms). Special Customers who first paid before the notice ends keep this special commission for the rest
of their 12 months, subject to section 7 of the Partner Terms.

4.2 If the partnership ends under the Partner Terms, section 7 of the Partner Terms applies to Special Customers.

## 5. General

5.1 This agreement and the Partner Terms are the whole agreement about your commission. Any change must be in writing
and signed by a director of Be More Swan Ltd and by you.

5.2 English law applies, and the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction, as in the Partner Terms.
