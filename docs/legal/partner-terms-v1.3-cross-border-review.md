# Partner Terms v1.3: cross-border review (UK / EU / US), 23 November 2026

**Why:** the earlier reviews were UK-centred. The owner asked for the contract to be reviewed for the UK, the EU and the US, by a legal counsel and by a financial-contract expert, each from **Be More Swan's side** and from **the partner's side**, with every applicable regulation reflected.

**Status:** the changes below are built on branch `feat/partner-terms-cross-border`. The version is still **1.3**: no partner has accepted any version, and v1.3 is not on prod yet, so no notice period applies.

## Owner decisions (23 November 2026)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| A partner who leaves, or turns down a change | **Same run-off as inactivity.** Standard (12 months or less) customers run to term; longer ones earn for 3 months. Only breach, fraud or sanctions endings stop everything (7.4). |
| Customer payments in USD / EUR | **Converted to sterling** at the provider's rate. This replaces "sterling only" (3.2). |
| Partners promoting competitors | **Allowed**, and said in 6.1. This strengthens "introducer, not agent" (EU) and contractor status (US AB5). |
| Liability caps | Our liability, in addition to paying earned commission, is the greater of **£500** and 12 months' commission. The partner's indemnity is the greater of **£10,000** and 12 months' commission, unlimited if the breach was deliberate, reckless or fraudulent (10.1, 10.2). |

## Code that the wording relies on (same branch)

- Payouts need *a* version accepted, not the current one.
- Run-off for `left`, `change_declined` and `closure`.
- Automatic `change_declined` ending 60 days after a change.
- 180-day clawback limit.
- Currency conversion via the Stripe balance transaction.
- Pro rata past the window, and the 60-day annual hold.
- Acceptance log table and copy email.
- Country and joining-as fields on the application.
- Hashed IP in the invite rate-limit key.
- VAT on top for UK VAT-registered partners, and a self-billed statement per payout.
- Sanctions reminder in admin.

SQL: `db/z-partner-terms-cross-border.sql` (both databases, before deploy).

## Not changed (deliberately)

- **English law and courts for business partners:** kept. Hague 2005 covers enforcement in the EU, and the US recognises judgments under the Uniform Act.
- **No arbitration or class-action waiver for US partners:** not recommended at launch, because of mass-arbitration fee risk. Reassess as US partner numbers grow.
- **Commission on gross receipts excluding tax, not net of card fees:** the difference is under 1.5 points, and gross is market-standard and checkable by partners.
- **CCPA, US sales-rep statutes, DAC7 and the Platform Operators Regulations:** reviewed and found not to apply today.

## Watch list

- **US click-through nexus:** New York presumes nexus at over $10,000 a year of receipts referred by NY-resident affiliates, and NY taxes SaaS. Track it once US partners exist.
- **EU AI Act Art. 50(2) machine-readable marking of AI output:** possibly from 2 December 2026, depending on the AI Omnibus. This is a product duty, outside these Terms.
- **GDPR Art. 27 EU representative:** decide (EU question 1 below).
- **Wise and PayPal fees:** confirm the PayPal sender fee and that the Wise batch delivers the full GBP amount.
- **Accounting:** expense commission as incurred under FRS 102/105, and record the liability when the customer pays. The accountant should confirm.

## Questions only qualified lawyers can answer

**England & Wales solicitor**
1. Does a SaaS subscription supplied as remote access count as "goods" under the Commercial Agents Regulations 1993, after *Software Incubator* [2024] UKSC 8? Does sharing a link amount to "negotiating"?
2. Do 7.4, 7.5, 4.3 and 10 pass UCTA s3/s11 and CRA Part 2, given the business-capacity statement in 2.1?
3. Is a contractual indemnity for Be More Swan's own regulatory fines enforceable after *Safeway v Twigger*?
4. Confirm the VAT Notice 700/62 self-billing requirements, and the reverse charge for supplies from EU and US partners.
5. Confirm that the Platform Operators Regulations 2023 do not apply.

**EU counsel (DE / FR / IE / NL)**
1. Does recruiting EU partners engage GDPR Art. 3(2)? If so, is an Art. 27 representative needed?
2. Are online affiliates *Handelsvertreter* or *agents commerciaux* after *Trendsetteuse* (C-828/18)? What would the §89b / L134-12 indemnity look like given the 7.4(a) run-off?
3. Are side-income affiliates consumers under Brussels I bis Art. 17 and Rome I Art. 6, after *Personal Exchange International* (C-774/19)?
4. Does the French influencer law (Loi 2023-451, as amended) catch affiliates?
5. Is the AI Omnibus adopted, and does the 2 December 2026 Art. 50(2) date apply?

**US attorney / CPA**
1. Does a UK company with no US trade or business, paying US partners through Wise or PayPal, have any 1099-NEC, backup-withholding or W-9 duty? What changes with a US subsidiary?
2. Are individual affiliates "freelance workers" under the NY, IL and CA statutes, and do the Terms plus payout statements satisfy their content rules? What is the AB5 prong-B exposure?
3. Is an arbitration clause or class-action waiver worth adding for US partners?
4. Is there any remaining TCPA or Washington CEMA exposure from partner emails or DMs?
